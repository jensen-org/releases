Jensen is a desktop application, with a `jensen` command beside it, for codebases too large to hold
in your head. It maps your project and hands that same map to the AI assistant you already use.

## Honest by design

Jensen never guesses. Everything on the map was either observed in your source or declared by your
team, and the two stay distinguishable.

[Read the guarantee](/releases/start/honest-by-design/)

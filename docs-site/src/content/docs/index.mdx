Jensen is a desktop app, with a `jensen` command beside it, for codebases too large to hold in your
head. It maps your project, gives that map to the AI assistant you already use, and runs the work
through one flow: plan, approve, build on a task branch, land.

## Honest by design

Jensen never guesses. Everything on the map was observed in your source or confirmed by you, and
the two stay distinguishable.

[Read the guarantee](/start/honest-by-design/)
